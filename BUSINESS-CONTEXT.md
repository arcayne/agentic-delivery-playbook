# Business context

## Current thesis

Agentic Delivery Playbook is a small, evidence-first kernel for teams using Codex, ChatGPT Work, and Pi on consequential repository changes. It may help people constrain scope, select a minimum safe GPT-6 route, and evaluate work from evidence rather than narration.

The kernel is the product hypothesis, not a promise of improved speed, cost, or quality. Routing may add value, or a consistent Sol baseline may be enough. [Protocol 1.0](docs/evaluation.md) is frozen historical GPT-5.6 evidence; it does not validate current GPT-6 routing. Evaluating GPT-6 requires a separately versioned protocol.

## Intended user

A senior or lead developer who already uses coding agents on real codebases and needs a lightweight way to make authority, acceptance, validation, and review explicit.

## Boundaries

In scope:

- Direct and Controlled delivery guidance
- Codex and ChatGPT Work adapters, plus a skill-only Pi package adapter
- GPT-6 routing guidance and separately versioned evaluation
- evidence-backed claims

Out of scope:

- a claim of provider-wide support
- a promise of unmeasured performance or cost savings
- unsupported historical material, which remains in [legacy/](legacy/README.md)

## Open questions

- Does the kernel reduce drift, defects, or human burden on representative tasks?
- Does explicit routing outperform a consistent Sol medium baseline?
- Which users will maintain and validate future adapter proposals?
