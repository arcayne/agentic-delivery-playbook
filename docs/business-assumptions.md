# Business assumptions

## Current hypothesis

The delivery kernel may help teams keep agent changes scoped, verifiable, and evidence-backed. Evaluation protocol 1.0 is frozen historical GPT-5.6 routing evidence; evaluating GPT-6 routing requires a separately versioned evaluation. The [evaluation protocol](evaluation.md) decides rather than marketing claims.

| ID | Assumption | Type | Current evidence | Next test | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A001 | Developers using Codex or ChatGPT Work will find a small delivery kernel useful for consequential changes. | problem | Internal use only; no external validation recorded. | Interview users and observe whether they retain the kernel. | open |
| A002 | The simplified kernel improves drift, defects, or human burden versus a no-playbook baseline. | delivery | Unmeasured. | Run a separately versioned evaluation; protocol 1.0 is frozen historical GPT-5.6 evidence. | open |
| A003 | Explicit Luna, Sol, and Astra routing improves total economics without reducing quality. | routing | Unmeasured. | Evaluate GPT-6 routing under a separately versioned evaluation. | open |

Do not describe the kernel as faster, cheaper, or higher quality until reproducible evaluation evidence supports that claim.
